== OpenShift Roadshow

During this 2 days, you will have the possibility to access to 2 different labs:

* **Beginner's lab:** A first introduction to OpenShift Virtualization focus on how a virtualization user can easily deploy VM via the OpenShift Console.
* **Advanced lab:** A more advanced guide focus on VM optimization and placement

*Feel free to follow the one you want.*


[WARNING]
====
Please, read attentively these different points before to start:


* The environments for these labs are generated on-demand and are not representative of reality. Indeed, for cost and automation reasons, these OpenShift Virtualization environments are "nested" and not supported in production. Performance may therefore be significantly affected.
* **Point 1 :** Assurez-vous de bien suivre les étapes dans l'ordre.
* **Point 2 :** L'environnement de lab est généré à la demande.

N'hésitez pas à vérifier vos identifiants avant de continuer.
====
